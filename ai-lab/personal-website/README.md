# 🍝 paragsalunkhe.me

**What it is.** My personal site, styled as a zine I hand people at the door: I host supper clubs and movie nights. It has movie reviews, recipes, blog posts, notes and a guestbook. → [paragsalunkhe.me](https://www.paragsalunkhe.me/)

**v1 (Oct 2025).** A full-stack web app built by someone who is neither a frontend nor a backend developer: me, a DevOps and cloud engineer, pairing with AI. The blog post on the site tells that story.

**v2 (in progress, not live yet): "one evening".**
- **The home page as one evening:** a tilt-shift street at dusk, a camera dolly through a window, a candle-lit table, then a turn into the projector beam.
- **Every section has its own scene:** movies on a spotlight poster wall, cooking as a pop-up book, blog posts as invitations with opening flaps, notes as place cards.
- **A motion lab of experiments** to pick directions from, including Blender-made scenes.
- **Security hardening:** locked-down write APIs, hardened sign-in, and security headers.
- **Evals and project skills** for the AI that works on the site: security audit, code review, systematic debugging, and verify-before-done.
- **Performance:** the movie wall went from 50/30 fps to 59/60 fps at 1440p, with less drawn off screen.

**Built with:** Next.js, Three.js, GSAP, Prisma, Blender, Claude Code.
